Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

SamjhaDo helps users understand documents, bills, images, PDFs and everyday questions in simple language. This policy explains how information may be handled when you use the website.

1. Information You Provide

When you use SamjhaDo, you may submit chat messages, questions, images, or text extracted from PDF documents. Please avoid submitting passwords, bank details, identity documents, or other sensitive personal information unless it is genuinely necessary.

2. How Your Information Is Used

Information you submit is used to generate responses, analyse supported uploads, and maintain recent conversation context so that follow-up questions can make sense.

3. Storage and Conversation History

Conversation messages may be processed by our backend and stored in a database to support conversation history. Recent messages from approximately the last seven days may be used as context for responses. This does not necessarily mean all stored records are automatically deleted after seven days.

4. AI Processing

SamjhaDo uses AI services to generate answers and analyse submitted content. Your submitted text or image data may be sent to the services required to process your request. AI responses can contain mistakes, so verify important financial, legal, educational, or official information independently.

5. Analytics and Technical Information

Website analytics may collect information about visits and website usage. Technical information may also be processed to operate, protect, and troubleshoot the service.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to operate the service, but no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Do not use SamjhaDo to store confidential information.

7. Third-Party Services

SamjhaDo relies on hosting, database, analytics, and AI infrastructure providers. Their services may process information according to their own policies and terms.

8. Children

If you are under the age required to consent to online data processing in your location, use the service with the involvement of a parent or guardian where required by law.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as SamjhaDo changes. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
