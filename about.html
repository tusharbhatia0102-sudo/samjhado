About SamjhaDo

SamjhaDo makes everyday information easier to understand.

Our goal is to help people understand confusing documents, bills, screenshots, PDFs and everyday questions in simple language.

What is SamjhaDo?

SamjhaDo is an online AI-powered assistant designed to make information easier to understand. You can ask questions, share supported images, or provide document text to get explanations in straightforward language.

What can you use it for?

  • Understanding bills and receipts.
  • Explaining notices, forms and documents.
  • Getting help with study questions and basic calculations.
  • Understanding screenshots and supported images.
  • Summarising information and asking follow-up questions.

Our approach

We aim to make the experience simple, accessible and useful. You should be able to ask a question in everyday language and receive an explanation that is easier to follow.

Important information

SamjhaDo uses AI, so answers may sometimes be incomplete or incorrect. Please verify important calculations and financial, legal, medical or official information with a reliable source. SamjhaDo is not a substitute for a qualified professional.
